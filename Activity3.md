## ˚⊱🪷⊰˚Creative Process & Design Choices

1. **Concept Development:** As a nursing student, I developed the concept for an **IT-Enabled HIV Prevention and Awareness System** to bridge public health education with digital technology. The project directly addresses key barriers identified by WHO guidelines—including stigma, discrimination, misinformation, and lack of awareness—to promote testing and preventive behaviors.
2. **Layout Planning:** Structured the content into 7 clear, numbered modules using a clean grid layout:
   * **Module 1 (What is HIV?):** Defines HIV/AIDS and treatment potential.
   * **Module 2 (Why is HIV still a problem?):** Highlights barriers like stigma and lack of awareness.
   * **Module 3 (Global Evidence):** Summarizes global statistical data.
   * **Module 4 (Transmission / DON'Ts):** Clarifies direct transmission routes (unprotected sex, needles, blood, mother-to-child).
   * **Module 5 (External / Risk Factors & Impact):** Outlines risk behaviors and systemic impacts on the body, mental health, and communities.
   * **Module 6 (Solution & Prevention):** Lists actionable preventive steps (condoms, testing, PrEP, PEP, sterile equipment).
   * **Module 7 (Core Advocacy Banner):** Emphasizes the main message: *"Know the Facts. Prevent the Spread. End the stigma."*
3. **Visual Execution:** Designed using high-contrast red callout blocks paired with soft pastel backgrounds to balance health urgency with an approachable look. Used clear icons (thumbs-down "DON'Ts" badge, checkmark solution lists) to guide the reader's eye seamlessly through key healthcare concepts.
